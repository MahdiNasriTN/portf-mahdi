Hi, I'm Nesil.

7+ years designing digital experiences, products and interfaces. Currently leading Product Design at upGrad, where I work across product, strategy, systems, AI and design culture.

Tokens become components. Components become products. Products become something a team can keep building on.

7+ years.

Still curious. Still making.

I build products and the teams that build products.

From the first blank Figma frame to a product people actually use. I’ve built products from scratch, shaped design systems, and built teams that make good ideas happen.

My obsession lives in the gap between usable and unforgettable.

I believe great design should solve the problem first, then make people feel something. I bring together product thinking, art direction and a little bit of the unexpected to create experiences that work beautifully and stay with you.

I design for inclusion.

Not because it's on a checklist, but because the most interesting design problems live at the edges, not the centre.

These days I'm also in the code.

Prototyping faster, helping designers push PRs, shipping alongside engineers. Not because it's trendy, but because the seam between design and engineering is where the craft either holds or falls apart.

I'd rather be standing there when it decides.

I don't believe in polite decks that protect average ideas.

I don't believe in vanilla.

I believe in things with a spine: products that are unmistakably themselves, built by people who give a damn about the details no one asked them to care about.

That's what I make.
That's what I lead.

If you're building something that deserves to be remembered, let's talk.

I'm open to building brands, designing experiences and good conversations over a cup of coffee.
